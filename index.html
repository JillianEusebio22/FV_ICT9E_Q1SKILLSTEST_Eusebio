before

Always make sure to warp up for 5-10 minutes before you dance, it prepares your body for the upcoming things you will do. It also helps prevent injuries. A tip for begginer's is to find a good spot to dance, preferably somewhere that is not so narrow so you will be able to dance without hitting your body.

during

Listen to the person teaching the choreohraphy and ask questions when needed, remember to go full out while you can but if you are having a hard time you can always mark out the steps first or better yet learn the countings, but most importantly have fun!

after

Cool down! Drink water and freshen up, if you are aiming to improve more, practice the steps at home so you can fully absorb the timing and movement of the dance. And always remember that it's ok to make mistakes in dance!
